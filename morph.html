what even happened

honestly, i didn't have a plan. just a hunch: put eight curious people in one house for three weeks and get out of the way, and something would happen. something did.

some days the whole house went quiet — everyone heads-down on their own thing at the kitchen table. other days nobody worked at all and we just talked until 3am. we cooked for each other most nights. people wrote, built weird little things, read on the sofa and argued about it over dinner.

it wasn't a retreat, or an incubator, or a co-living startup. it was just us, trying to make things next to each other for a bit. [i'll write the real version of this soon, promise]

i run bigger things than this — an unconference, hundreds of people. this is the one i actually think about. — me, after, still thinking about it
